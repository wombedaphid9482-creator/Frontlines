# Frontlines v1.0.3 — Balance Recovery

[Windows installer](../release/1.0.3/Frontlines-Setup-1.0.3.exe) · [Installation-free app](../release/1.0.3/win-unpacked/Frontlines.exe). Keep the complete unpacked folder together. This is a local candidate; it has not been published or installed over the owner's copy.

The patch continues the latest v1.0.2 viewport hotfix. Scavenge and the Scavenger Commander's Nothing Wasted share one casualty draw per player/global turn; Pull Back and Ghost Extraction retain wounds through redeployment. Drifter and Recover the Fallen are unchanged. No card's printed Presence, Attack, Health or Command Action costs change.

Fortified Advance gains movable defensive bodies, Rolling Breakthrough gains a lighter Rush opening, and Coordinated Removal/Planned Exposure gain usable payoffs rather than extra setup. Saved custom lists remain intact. AI corrections address exact Commander costs/state, follow-up sequencing, capture pressure, dangerous unsupported advances and wounded reclaim valuation. The [complete change log](balance/SPRINT-010-CHANGELOG.md) names every changed card, Commander, preset and policy with BEFORE / AFTER / WHY / DATA / INTENDED BEHAVIOR.

Legal cards, legal targets and Commander availability have explicit visual states. Unavailable cards remain inspectable with blocking reasons; reclaimed wounds are visible. Random Enemy chooses a legal faction/deck/Commander, Random Deck preserves faction/Commander, and Avoid Last Opponent persists locally. War Room remains accessible within the game.

All original artwork assets and mappings, including the ten painted Commander portraits, match the preserved v1.0.2 baseline. Full card faces use a consistent 5:7 ratio with uniformly scaled, safely cropped painted surfaces. The populated battlefield still fits all seven territories at the required desktop sizes.

Validation: **334/334 Node tests pass**, including sixteen new recovery cases, eleven new AI cases, opponent/profile checks and two deterministic seat-paired integration fixtures. The battlefield passes thirteen viewport/scaling cases; action/card presentation passes ten contexts; random-opponent setup, avoidance, persistence and Lab migration pass browser checks. The complete fourteen-lesson tutorial passes at six viewports, including Commander teaching, training victory, loss/retry and progress persistence. Legacy presentation passes five resolutions, original art/symbol checks, deployment/cancellation and all ten portraits. Packaged native shell checks pass tutorial deployment, guided Commander activation, version display, F11 fullscreen and Alt+Enter return to windowed. The actual package matches all 95 tested runtime source files and excludes development/source-art material; all 31 frozen Sprint 5 files remain unchanged. Exact evidence is recorded in the release manifest.

Balance evidence is **interim 46,679/100,000**, explicitly approved by the owner: 46,402 decisive games, 277 cutoffs, zero reported errors. Verified rule/AI defects motivate this patch; these tests do not establish new win rates. **No large post-patch campaign was run.** [Sprint 10](SPRINT-010.md#exact-recommended-owner-run-validation--not-executed) gives the exact owner-run 100,000-match recommendation: seed 1209, all thirty baseline decks, all Commanders, paired seats, mirrors, deck/deck AI, unchanged opening rules, and the `sprint10` profile. [Machine-readable options](balance/sprint10-validation-options.json) can also be checked against the frozen baseline before starting a run.

Both seats have equal resources/draws on their own first initiative. Player 1 can accrue capture first, and immediate conquest ends before another reply; this remains a structural first-mover risk. No unverified compensation or turn-system rewrite was added. Rogue, the four repaired presets and strong non-Rogue decks still require owner-run validation and human playtesting; tactical expansion and multiplayer remain postponed.

One packaged ordinary-match smoke also completes from setup to victory with both Commander actives used (18 turns, 108 decisions); its outcome is a correctness check, not a competitive estimate.
